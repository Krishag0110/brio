## Original two-person split and integrated contracts

The [PRD section 16 ownership and milestone tables](PRD.md#16-two-person-implementation-plan) remain the design's two-person implementation split. Builder A's product/workflow boundary maps to `src/core`, `src/control`, `src/shared/control-contract.ts`, UI, Convex state/auth/actions, approvals, persona evaluation and the ledger. Builder B's execution boundary maps to `workers/shared`, `workers/social`, `workers/engineering`, the independent weather repository, protected tests, provider protocols and restricted Actions jobs.

Integrated boundaries are explicit: public snapshot/actions in `src/shared/control-contract.ts`; durable task/actor/state in `src/control/types.ts`; approval/evidence rules in `src/core`; signed job/grant/result/session schemas in `workers/shared`; Convex worker/release mutations consume them. Provider, HTTP, worker-control, release-control and browser tests exercise both sides. Source integration does not invent two human builders' qualitative sign-off or real M2/M3 receipts.

## Remaining acceptance gates

- Retain the completed local production/browser and truthful-workaround evidence with the submitted revision; retain the corrected successful durable triage and unresolved historical reservations; retain the completed live persona metrics and transparent callback-recovery note.
- Execute a generated candidate in the reviewed restricted runtime, retain pass-after protected checks, and link actual PR/staged/live identities. The baseline proves the deliberate defect, not the fix.
- Configure approved integration accounts and hosted access, then retain real signed Slack, Linear, GitHub/Vercel and social receipts. Reddit publication is conditional; controlled manual URL intake is supported, automated Reddit ingestion is absent.
- Obtain the specified qualitative review and actual Slack approval before calling the evaluated standing policy active.
- Measure state visibility within five seconds, eligible engagement within 60 seconds, engineering within 15 minutes excluding human/provider waits, and Slack acknowledgement below three seconds. Polling intervals and timeouts are not measured end-to-end results.

Finite tests establish observed behavior for covered inputs and environments. They cannot prove all future inputs, provider behavior, races, deployments, or cost exposure correct.
