# brio — 100-second voice-over and edit sheet

Read the continuous script naturally. The one-second rows are placement guides for the editor, not separate takes or instructions to speak mechanically. Quoted customer/approval text can be read by the same narrator; no synthetic character voices are needed.

## Continuous reading script

Three customers are reporting the same problem with Drizzle.

“Twenty Celsius becomes twenty Fahrenheit.” “Same here.” “Please fix the toggle.”

brio groups those reports into one incident, keeping each original post attached.

The QA agent reproduces the bug. Twenty Celsius should be sixty-eight Fahrenheit.

Ellen gets the evidence and proposed change in Slack. She reviews the scope and clicks Build.

Now brio starts the fix. The same card moves through engineering, with the patch and test results attached.

The candidate passes its checks. In the preview, twenty Celsius now converts to sixty-eight Fahrenheit.

David reviews the proposed customer reply alongside the evidence. He approves Go, authorizing this candidate and this exact message.

brio releases the approved change, checks the live version, and confirms the conversion works before replying.

Back on X, the original customer gets the update. The reply receipt closes the case, with both human decisions recorded.

But “Drizzle, please make it rain” isn’t a bug. That’s a job for the brand’s personality.

The approved playful persona keeps it short: “bruh.”
